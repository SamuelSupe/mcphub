Who this guide is for

This guide is for employees using company tools from their own computers and people reviewing write requests. Deployment, identity services, tool publication, and operations are covered in the administrator manual.

The guide covers MCPHub v2.2.1. Your company may enable only some services; your authorization page shows the actual tools, permissions, and approval requirements. hub.example.com, ci_example, and service names are examples. Replace them with your actual values.
